Organize Layers into persistent, independently collapsible Front and Back artboards with compact artwork stacks and native ink entries. Activate an artboard by selecting its artwork, double-click its header to fit it, and move detached artwork or folders between artboards with undo while retaining their physical frames and descendants. Keep shared properties and history controls in one place.
